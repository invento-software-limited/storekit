# Changelog

## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-18

### ✨ Added
- Invento branding for the storefront: all Builder pages and components (navbar, footer, home, about, contact, products, product page, case studies, checkout, account, policy and payment pages) now use Invento Software Limited content in place of the template text.
- Hero images on every page: photos from invento.com.bd on the main pages, and a brand SVG pattern on the policy, account, checkout and payment pages.
- "Trusted by" section with client logos, and Frappe Bronze Partner, Odoo Ready, BASIS and e-CAB badge images in the footer.
- Brand assets and 40 product images (1000×1000 WebP) in `public/builder_assets/invento/`.
- Invento favicon.
- Variant picker on the product page. Products with options link to the product page from the product cards. The cart API rejects template and unpublished items.
- "On Sale" filter, sale and stock badges, and a breadcrumb on the product page.
- Product reviews for logged-in customers.
- Patch `storekit.patches.v1_1.remove_unused_custom_fields` removes unused Item Group, Item and Sales Order custom fields and their property setters.

### 🐛 Fixed
- Product sorting now runs before paging, so page 2 continues the order of page 1.
- The guest checkout total now uses the delivery postcode, so the total shown is the total charged.
- A guest cart is now kept, merged only once and priced correctly when the shopper logs in. The merge uses a database lock.
- After login, shoppers return to the shop, not to `/desk`.
- Shipping VAT now uses the company's own tax accounts, not fixed account names.
- Checkout now works on a BDT site:
  - Bank Transfer is accepted.
  - The payment method and quotation reference are saved on the Sales Order.
  - Digital delivery is free.
- Parallel cart and address requests no longer fail with "has been modified" (HTTP 417).
- Profile address edits now save, and failed saves show an error.
- Category links now work for group names that contain "&".
- The contact form accepts a country that is not in the Country list.
- The ৳ sign now has a font (Noto Sans Bengali).
- The `url(transparent)` requests (404s) are gone, and closed drawers no longer leave a shadow.

### 🎨 Changed
- Products with options show "From <price>". Prices for count units (Nos, Unit, Each, Pcs, Piece) no longer end in "per Nos".
- The checkout confirmation no longer promises shipping in 5–7 business days.
- Case-study covers use a 3:2 ratio, and card images in a grid have the same height.
- Checkout asks for "District" instead of "County / State".
- Merged the fixes from `version-16` up to 1.2.3.
